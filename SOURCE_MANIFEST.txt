JARVIS Android adaptation source set

Primary sources:
- Jarvis.zip
- Downloads.zip
- JARVIS_FINAL_PACKAGE_V3.zip
- JARVIS_FAST_BUILD.zip

Runtime result:
- JARVIS_HOST: Windows JARVIS with Ollama-only config + LAN bridge.
- JARVIS_ANDROID: native Android client.

The legacy download artifacts are preserved under LEGACY_DOWNLOADS/ and LEGACY_PACKAGES/ for traceability; they are not loaded by the Android runtime.
